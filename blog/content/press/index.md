## Wordle Reverse Engineering & Secondary Press Coverage

*Back when Wordle was everywhere, I accidentally played a day's puzzle without my partner, Julianna. She was not pleased! As an olive branch, I reverse-engineered the client-side game logic and built "Wordle Time Machine." By monkey-patching JavaScript's Date object, I exposed the full 2,315-word solution array (including "Day 0"), allowing anyone to play any past or future game by calendar date.  Turns out this was something people really wanted: I casually tweeted about it which triggered an organic wave of coverage across (medium)stream tech press.*

1. [**Original Post: Reverse Engineering Wordle**](https://mottaquikarim.github.io/dev/posts/reverse-engineering-wordle/) (2022-01) (mottaquikarim.github.io) The core technical post dissecting Wordle's client-side JavaScript bundle, extracting the array of 2,315 solutions, and mapping date offset logic back to June 19, 2021. It's a technical but fun read, if I do say so myself.
2. [**Wordle Time Machine Application**](https://mottaquikarim.github.io/wordle_timemachine/) (2022-01) (mottaquikarim.github.io) The (still!) functional tool built on top of the teardown: an interactive web application allowing users to play past or future Wordle puzzles by calendar date.
3. **Nerdschalk** (2022-01): [*Wordle Unlimited Games*](https://nerdschalk.com/wordle-unlimited-games-play-wordle-more-than-once-a-day-and-more-with-these-tips/) (nerdschalk.com) Feature article crediting the reverse-engineering of all 2,315 stock words, citing the "Day 0" archive, and embedding my original launch tweet, the first of four independent outlets to cover this unprompted.
4. **Nerdschalk** (2022-02): [*6 Ways to Play Old Wordle Puzzles*](https://nerdschalk.com/5-ways-to-play-old-wordle-puzzles-on-your-pc-and-phone/) (nerdschalk.com) Follow-up step-by-step consumer guide recommending Wordle Time Machine as a primary archive resource.
5. **TwoAverageGamers** (2022-04): [*How to Play Old Wordle Puzzles*](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/how-to-play-old-wordle-puzzles/) (twoaveragegamers.com) Media feature highlighting the "Unofficial Wordle Time Machine by Taq Karim."
6. **Alphr** (2022-07): [*How to Play Old Wordle Games*](https://www.alphr.com/play-old-wordle-games/) (alphr.com) Tech guide recommending the archive as a top method for playing past puzzles, a third independent outlet, five months after launch, meaning durable interest rather than a one-day spike.
